I'm a **software engineer with 4+ years of experience** across Python, backend engineering, data automation, enterprise integrations, and production support. I enjoy working at the intersection of **business needs and hands-on engineering**: understanding the problem, building a practical solution, integrating it into real workflows, and improving it through feedback.

- 🧭 **What I focus on:** Forward Deployed Engineering · Applied AI · Agentic AI
- 🧩 **What I build:** API-driven applications, RAG assistants, tool-using agents, automation workflows
